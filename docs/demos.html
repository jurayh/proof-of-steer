The Steerprint

The counterfactual shadow

The shipped artifact with only the lines that survive from the first prompt lit up. The lit part is what the first prompt built. Everything dark arrived in later turns.

In the steered session only 398 of 3,102 lines survive from the first prompt. In the one-prompt control the shadow equals the artifact, because nothing arrived after the first prompt. Line authorship calls both artifacts identical. The shadow shows they are not.

The artifact replay

A time-lapse of the artifact being built, turn by turn. Files appear as they are created. Strips fill as lines arrive, shift color as later turns reshape them, and fade as lines are discarded. Play it, pause it, scrub through it, and watch forty turns of steering build an app.

The replay has 41 frames, one for the empty artifact and one after each human turn, with play, pause, scrub, and speed controls. The final frame matches the receipt exactly at 3,102 lines across 28 files.

The rework web

Which turns talked to which. Forty turns sit on a timeline in order, arranged in four lanes by turn kind, so kind reads by position as well as by color, and an arc runs from each later turn back to the earlier turn whose lines it removed. The shape shows the session arguing with itself: most arcs land in the first half of the session, and the longest ones reach back to the opening turns. Turn 6 removed 340 lines from turn 4, the single biggest rewrite, and turn 14 removed 713 lines from earlier turns, more than any other turn. A 3D spiral of the same web sits behind a toggle.

The dual timeline

Two lanes on one time axis. The top lane is the human, each turn shaped by its kind, with decision dots where they answered the agent's questions. The bottom lane is the artifact, lines after each turn. A correction in the top lane lines up with the dip it caused below. Turn 6 is the biggest dip, net minus 40 lines, and the decisions cluster at the end, on turns 32, 33, and 34.
