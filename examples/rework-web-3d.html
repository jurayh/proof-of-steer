Each node is one human turn, placed left to right in turn order and in the lane for its kind, so kind reads by position as well as by color. An edge from a later turn to an earlier turn means the later turn removed lines the earlier turn introduced. Edge thickness grows with the number of lines removed. Long arcs reach far back in time. Short arcs join neighbouring turns. The same graph in 3D sits behind the 3D spiral toggle. Edges count discards in the replay. They are not a claim about what the author meant. The one-prompt control has a single turn and therefore no rework to show.

The graph comes from the shipped prefix replays, the same snapshots the artifact replay uses. For each turn, the lines of each earlier turn present before the turn but absent after it become one edge, weighted by the count. Counts cover non-blank lines only, matching the receipt.
